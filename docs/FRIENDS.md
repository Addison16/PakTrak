# Wishlist, friends and trade offers

## Wishlist

**Menu → Wishlist** keeps the cards you're looking for, with a finish (any, nonfoil, foil or etched), a quantity and a reference price from your chosen price source. The total shows what the list costs, and **Buy your wishlist** opens TCGplayer, Card Kingdom or ManaPool with the list filled in, the same way a deck's buy list does.

Add cards by searching, by pasting a list (`2 Lightning Bolt` or `1 Sol Ring (C21) 263`, one per line), with **Add missing cards to wishlist** in a deck's buy list, or from **Set completion**. Imported files that mark rows as a wishlist or list keep those rows out of your collection; the import's page offers **Add them to my wishlist**. Each entry also shows how many copies of that card you already own.

## Friends

Friends can see each other's collections and wishlists and send trade offers. PakTrak never lists who has an account on the server, and there is no search for people.

- Each account can create a **friend code** under **Menu → Friends**. It looks like `ABCDE-23456` and is random, so it can't be guessed from a name.
- To add someone, enter the code they gave you. They see a request and decide whether to accept. You don't see their name until they do.
- A wrong code always gets the same answer, whether or not the account exists. After 10 wrong codes in an hour, PakTrak asks you to wait.
- **Get a new code** stops the old one working; current friends stay connected. **Turn off code** means no one can send you a request.
- **What friends can see** lets you stop sharing your collection or wishlist at any time. Storage locations and notes are never shared.
- **Remove** ends the friendship for both people and cancels offers still waiting.

Opening a friend shows the cards they have that are on your wishlist, the cards you have that are on theirs, their wishlist and a searchable view of their collection.

### Friends on other PakTrak servers

When your administrator has connected this PakTrak to another PakTrak server (see [connecting to other PakTrak servers](OPERATIONS.md#connecting-to-other-paktrak-servers)), you can be friends with people there too. Enter their code with their server's address after an `@`, for example `ABCDE-23456@cards.example.net`. The Friends page shows your own code in that form for people on other servers.

Everything else works the same way: they decide whether to accept, a wrong code gets the same answer, and you see their name only after they accept. Friends on other servers show their server's address under their name. Their collection and wishlist follow their own sharing switches, and storage locations and notes are never shared. Trade offers are only between people on the same server for now.

## Trade offers

From a friend's page, or by choosing them under **Trading with** on **Trade value**, add the cards you give and the cards you get, then **Send offer**. The other person sees the offer at the top of their Home screen and under **Menu → Trade offers**, with the count of offers waiting for them.

When they accept, PakTrak checks they still have what they give, then updates **their** collection: the cards they get are added to a **Trades** binder as an import (so it can be undone from Import / export), and the cards they give are removed. Your own app updates your collection the next time you open it, from the notice on Home or **Update my collection** under Trade offers. **I already updated it** marks the trade done without changing anything, for trades you've already recorded by hand.

Either person can decline or cancel a waiting offer. Each person sees the offer priced with their own price source and today's prices.

## Price history and collection value

When daily prices update, PakTrak saves the price of each printing that someone owns or wants, and each person's collection value, for about 400 days. **Collection → Value over time** charts your collection's value with the change over the chosen period. A card's details show its own price history for the selected finish, and **In your decks** lists the decks using that card with how many copies are free for trades or another deck.

History starts on the first price update after this version is installed; the charts need two days of prices.

## Set completion

**Menu → Set completion** lists every set you own cards from, with how many of its collector numbers you have. Opening a set lists the missing cards, the cost to finish it with the cheapest finish of each, and **Add missing cards to wishlist**.
