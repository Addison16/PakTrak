## Implemented

- Public hostname changes retain existing account owners through a pinned local identity-realm binding. Startup synchronizes login destinations and migrates the issuer while preserving roles, quotas and all owned data. Unknown or conflicting identities stop startup, and a callback from a process with an outdated address cannot create a duplicate guest. See [changing the public hostname](OPERATIONS.md#https-and-access-from-a-phone).

- Saved decks now show **Deck value** with an account-saved choice of TCGplayer, Card Kingdom or ManaPool, counted duplicates, section subtotals, finish preferences and a filterable unpriced breakdown. Values use cached exact-edition quotes, clearly identify missing prices and outdated data, and update with valid draft edits without modifying inventory. See [deck values](DECKS.md#deck-value).

- **Scan a deck** creates a named deck from multiple linked photos; existing decks have **Scan cards**. Deck-only photos confirm matches without adding inventory, with an upload option to also add collection copies. Preview images, commander/mainboard/sideboard choices, pending-match exclusion, per-source duplicate protection and safe retries lead into the saved deck gallery. Scans retain server processing and lifetime quotas; earlier collection batches can also supply deck cards. See [physical deck scanning](DECKS.md#scanning-a-physical-deck).

- Browser **Back/Forward** and supported phone swipe-back gestures follow the main sections, deck/scan views, card previews and managed user accounts. Back dismisses an open menu or camera first. Existing unsaved-edit, temporary-password and upload protections apply to history traversal, and authenticated reads restore saved records after a refresh without repeating mutations. Collection filters remain in memory between sections. See [browser navigation](NAVIGATION.md).

- **My account** provides display-name editing, secure password changes, scan allowance/history, collection counts, appearance and sign-out of other devices. **Administration → User management** lists/searches all accounts with per-user password resets, suspension/restoration, session revocation, scan pauses and configurable lifetime card caps. Resets provide a temporary password, end app/provider sessions and require a new password at sign-in. Approval defaults to unlimited member scanning; caps can be raised or removed without resetting usage. Server checks serialize concurrent card charges and recheck changing policy during uploads. Existing cards/decks are preserved, administrator access is protected, and version checks plus private audit records cover changes. See [accounts](ACCOUNTS.md).

- Saved decks appear as **two deck boxes per row on narrow phones and three on larger screens**, with colors from the whole mainboard/commander color identity. Commander decks feature their leader or both partners; other formats feature a mainboard card, preferring nonlands with more copies. Compact matte cases have beveled walls, thin hinged lids, large recessed artwork windows, discreet branding and original SVG color symbols, with aligned names, formats and counts. Saved finishes, emblems, multicolor blends, empty/missing-art placeholders and accessible labels remain available. Art crops share the authenticated catalog-image cache; full deck contents still load on opening. See [deck plans](DECKS.md).

- **Tokens for this deck** at the bottom of saved decks and the editor, with artwork previews, stats, abilities, source-card links, distinct token variants and sideboard/Commander-extra labels. Tokens and emblems are derived from the local catalog, update with draft/imported/saved card lists and remain separate from deck counts, collection quantities and buy lists. Missing details, failed lookups and arbitrary-copy limitations remain explicit. See [deck tokens](DECKS.md#tokens-for-the-deck).

- Administrator-controlled **Enhanced scanning**, disabled by default, retries difficult cards with a temporary 1200×1680 crop and CPU text cleanup. Standard recognition remains the baseline; weaker or failed extra passes retain it, and strong matches skip extra work. The setting persists, applies to upcoming steps without a restart, and is independent of guest signup. Original photos and saved crop sizes stay unchanged; no GPU or additional model is required. See [scan enhancement](SCANNING.md#optional-enhanced-scanning).

- Collector-number search shortcuts such as `Plains #287` work for every card type in scan approval, printing corrections, import repair, collection searches and deck pickers/filtering. Numbers match exactly, including letter/symbol suffixes, with set selection for repeated numbers across editions. The printing picker explains the shortcut, synchronizes its separate number field and offers edition filters even without a previously chosen printing. Searches remain local to the cached catalog; selection still requires the normal approval/save action.

- An in-app camera view with an available-camera selector, a device-local preference, optional framing guides, large bottom capture controls, actual image dimensions, enlarged photo inspection, review/retake and explicit Upload & scan. Switching releases the previous camera and refreshes light/zoom controls; an unavailable choice falls back to Automatic with a notice. Native Phone camera and Library routes, permission/error recovery, background pause, track cleanup and retrying the retained capture use the existing durable upload path. Optional still-photo capture falls back to the actual video frame with a visible explanation. See [camera capture](CAMERA.md).
