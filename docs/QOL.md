# Quality of life upgrades

## Pick up where you left off

Unfinished photos, card reviews, foil choices, crop corners, deck edits and deck imports can be restored on the same browser and account. Drafts expire after seven days. Restore banners let you inspect the saved inputs or discard them. Browser storage failures show a notice and leave editing available. Photos are removed only after durable server acceptance or deliberate discard; a different tab’s newer photo is preserved.

## Review scans comfortably

Open scan images in the enlarged viewer, switch between the crop and suggested artwork, and zoom. Crop editing supports zoom and arrow keys; Shift moves a corner farther. Review filters show cards needing attention, missing matches or finish conflicts. Sorting changes their display order while preserving physical card numbers. Saving foil choices keeps other printing and condition edits. Catalog searches keep explicit filters as you type and include artwork.

## Organize your collection

Search, filters, sort and gallery/list layout are remembered on this device and included in the page URL. Save named views or bookmark the URL. Card links load independently of the current gallery page.

Choose **Select cards**, select up to 100 printings across pages, and use **Organize selected**. Preview the exact copies before moving them or setting their finish. A storage-location filter limits the operation to that location.

Within **Manage copies**, move a chosen quantity, update condition and notes, or correct the printing and finish. Partial moves preserve source metadata and import history. Undoing the source import removes its remaining copies from every destination. Each scanned card represents one physical copy and cannot be split.

## Build and import decks

Deck quantity buttons and **Undo last edit** make adjustments easier. Ownership and missing-copy counts are recalculated while editing; loading and failure states stay explicit. Recover unfinished edits after reload. Conflicts keep your draft and show the latest saved deck before you choose to save again. Duplicate a saved deck to try another build or copy its complete list.

Deck import previews offer quantity and section corrections, issue filtering, printing selection and exclusion. Reviewed choices survive rebuilding the preview and reloading, including when the source text has changed. Existing decks keep their collection-matching preference. Collection imports have **Needs attention only**, **Previous issue** and **Next issue** controls with original source row numbers.

## Back up and restore

See [operations](OPERATIONS.md#upgrades-and-backups) for backup verification, scheduling and restore drills. Restores require a fresh project and empty directory. After partial moves exist, downgrading the database requires restoring a pre-upgrade backup; the migration refuses to discard split provenance.
